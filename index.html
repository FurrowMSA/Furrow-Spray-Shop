The Greens Keeper range

Two new Greens Keeper sprayers, purpose-built for sensitive turf — bowling and golf greens, grass tennis courts, cricket pitches and more. Spray chemical and trace-element solutions with confidence.

  • Smooth-tread pneumatic tyres. Minimise turf impact on greens, tennis courts and cricket pitches.
  • Air induction nozzles. Larger, air-filled droplets that dramatically reduce spray drift.
  • Optional Spot-On Foam Marker Kit. Lay a clean foam guide line so you never double-up or miss a strip.
